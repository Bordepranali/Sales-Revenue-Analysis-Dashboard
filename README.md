# Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales & Revenue Analysis Dashboard** built using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard analyzes sales performance, revenue trends, profitability, product performance, regional performance, and customer segments to generate meaningful business insights.

## Project Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
- Track important business KPIs
- Identify top-performing products
- Analyze revenue across categories and regions
- Understand customer segment contribution
- Identify monthly revenue trends
- Provide interactive filtering for business analysis

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

- **Total Revenue:** 2.30M
- **Total Profit:** 286.40K
- **Total Orders:** 5K
- **Total Quantity:** 38K
- **Average Order Value:** 458.61

## Dashboard Features

The dashboard provides:

- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Top 10 Products by Revenue
- Revenue by Category
- Revenue by Region
- Revenue by Customer Segment
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value
- Interactive filters for:
  - Order Date
  - Region
  - Category
  - Customer Segment

## Dataset

The dashboard is based on a sales dataset containing information about orders, products, categories, regions, customer segments, sales, quantity, and profit.

The data was imported into Power BI and prepared for analysis and visualization.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI**
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV**
- **DAX**
- **Data Visualization**

## Methodology

The project followed these major steps:

1. Imported the sales dataset into Power BI.
2. Prepared and organized the data for analysis.
3. Created calculated measures using DAX.
4. Designed KPI cards for important business metrics.
5. Created charts for revenue trends and category, region, product, and segment analysis.
6. Added interactive slicers and filters.
7. Analyzed the dashboard to identify important business insights.

## Business Insights

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- Technology generates the highest revenue among the product categories.
- The West region contributes the highest revenue among the regions.
- Consumer customers contribute the largest share of revenue.
- Monthly revenue varies over time, helping identify stronger and weaker sales periods.
- The Top 10 Products analysis highlights products contributing significantly to overall revenue.
- The dashboard allows users to monitor revenue, profit, orders, and quantity using interactive filters.
